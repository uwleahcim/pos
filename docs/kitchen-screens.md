# Kitchen screens

A screen at a station in the kitchen that lists what the station has to make. Each ticket is the dishes of
one send of an order, in the words the paper ticket uses, coloured by how long it has waited. A cook taps a
dish when it is made, and the ticket leaves when its last dish is.

It is any device that runs the app: a tablet on the wall by the wok, a Mac with a television on it, a
phone. Nobody is signed in on it. It is off on every device to start, and **the paper tickets print exactly
as they do today**: a screen is beside the printer, not in its place.

One more screen is for whoever calls the orders: **the pass**. It shows each ticket whole, every station's
dishes together, with what each station has made (see "The pass"). And every kitchen screen has **All day**:
how many of each dish its tickets still wait for (see "All day").

What the cooks mark reaches the people who carry the food: a dish that is made says **ready** on its check
and on its table on the floor map (see "What the servers see").

A kitchen screen **chimes when a ticket comes in**, the way a printer is heard across the kitchen (see "The
sound").

What the cooks mark also adds up to a report for the manager, **how long the kitchen takes**: by station,
hour, day and dish, over any days (`kitchen-times.md`).

## What a cook sees

The station's name at the top with how many tickets are waiting, and under it the tickets, **the longest in
the kitchen first**, read across and then down. A phone shows them one under the other; a tablet shows as
many side by side as fit.

Each ticket has:

- **Whose it is**: "Table 12", "Tab Priya", or a to-go order's number and name, "#14 Linda". Under it the
  server, the guests and the course of a table, or "Phone · ready 18:20" of a to-go order.
- **The minutes it has been in the kitchen**, and its colour: plain to start, **amber** after 10 minutes,
  **red** after 20. A manager sets the two numbers (see "The colours").
- **An allergy told on the order**, in a red band before anything else, and again under the dish of the
  seat it was told for.
- **The customer's note**, in a dark band under any allergy: what the customer wrote for the whole order
  on the website (`online-orders.md`), whole however long it is, as the paper has it under CUSTOMER'S NOTE.
  It is the customer's own words, not an allergy somebody picked: read it.
- **Also at**: the other stations cooking for the same send, as the paper says it.
- **Its dishes**, as the station's paper ticket words them: how many, the kitchen's name of the dish, its
  choices (a choice on a choice stands further in), the note, the seat. A part of a combination says "with
  Pu Pu Platter". The choices a station does not read (`station-choices.md`) are left off, as on its paper.
- **VOID**, white on black as on paper, on a dish voided after it was sent, with its name struck out.
- **CHANGED** on a dish changed after it was sent.

## Marking what is made

- **Tap a dish** when it is made: it gets a green tick and goes grey. Tap it again to take that back.
- **Done** marks every dish of the ticket at once.
- When the last dish of a ticket is marked, **the ticket leaves the screen**.
- A **voided dish** stays on its ticket, struck out, until the cook taps it: that is "seen". A dish that
  was already marked made when it was voided does not come back.
- **Recall**, at the top, lists the last ten tickets the station finished. **Bring back** puts one back
  among the tickets, where it was.

A mark is the kitchen's own word. **It changes no dish, no price and no payment, prints nothing, and is not
a step in the check's history.** The servers' screens show nothing of it yet (see "Not built").

## All day

**All day**, at the top of a station's screen and of the pass, lists every dish the screen's tickets still
wait for with how many, the most first: "4 Egg Roll, 2 General Tso Chicken". **Back** returns to the
tickets.

- A dish marked made is counted no longer; nor is a voided one. At the pass a dish counts until it went
  out.
- The count is by the dish's name alone: two General Tso Chicken, one of them extra spicy, are 2. The
  tickets say which.
- It follows the kitchen while it is looked at, and goes back to the tickets when nothing is left to count.

## The pass

The screen of whoever calls the orders and sends the plates out. Each ticket is **one send of an order with
the dishes of every station on it**, the longest in the kitchen first, coloured by its minutes like a
station's.

- Each dish says **where it is cooked**, at its right: "Wok", "Fryer". Once that station has marked it
  made, the name turns green with a tick.
- When every station has made its part, the ticket says **All made** in a green band under its head: it
  is ready to go.
- **Tap a dish** when it went out: it gets the green tick. Tap again to take that back. **Sent out** says
  it for the whole ticket, and the ticket leaves the pass.
- **Recall** lists the last ten tickets sent out, and **Bring back** returns one.
- A voided dish stays, struck out, until the pass taps it, as at a station.
- **A dish that went out is made at its station too**: it comes off the station's screen without the cook
  tapping it. If the station brings its ticket back afterwards, the station's later word stands.
- The pass does not wait for the stations: a ticket can be sent out whether or not they marked anything.
- A to-go order is on the pass once, with the dishes its stations cook. What the packer lists at the
  takeout station (the whole order, for the bag) is the packer's own screen.
- Like every mark, "went out" changes no dish, no price and no payment, and prints nothing. A to-go order
  is still marked Ready by the staff.

**Turning it on.** On the device at the pass: **Settings › Kitchen screen**, turn on **This screen is the
pass**, then **Save**. With no station picked it watches every station. Pick stations to watch those alone:
a kitchen's pass with the bar left out shows no ticket for a round of drinks. A screen is a station's or
the pass, not both; to make it a station's screen again turn the switch off.

## What the servers see

Since 18 November 2026. What the cooks tap reaches the screens on the floor, so nobody walks to the kitchen
to ask whether table 12's food is up. Nothing is turned on for it: it shows wherever a kitchen screen marks
dishes, and a kitchen that works off paper alone sees no change at all.

**A dish is ready when every station that cooks it has marked it made.** A dish of the wok alone is ready
when the wok taps it. A dish cooked at two stations is not ready while one of them still works on it.

**On the check**, under the dish:

- **ready 18:50**, in green: the kitchen has it made and it waits to be carried.
- **went out 18:52**, in plain grey: it was carried to the table.
- **Wok ready · waiting on Sushi bar**: a dish of two stations with one part made.
- "sent 18:42, 23 min ago" stops counting once the dish is ready and says "sent 18:42": how long the table
  has waited matters while it waits.

**Over the check**, a green strip while dishes wait to be carried: "Ready: Peking duck, 2 × Fried rice".
It is over the check and the menu alike, so on a phone it shows on both tabs.

- **Went out**, on the strip, says the dishes listed were carried to the table. They then say "went out",
  the strip goes, and the bar at the bottom offers **Undo** for a tap that was a slip.
- It is the same word the pass says at its own screen, so a dish a server marks leaves the pass's screen
  too, and one the pass sends out leaves the server's strip. A kitchen with no screen at the pass has the
  servers say it here.
- Anyone on the check can tap it. No PIN is asked.

**On the floor map**, a green badge on the table's lower edge: **2 ready**. The countdown's badge is on the
upper edge, so a table can wear both. With **Mine** on, the list beside the map says "2 dishes ready" on the
table's row, and another server's badge fades with their table.

**How long it shows.** The badge and the strip go when the dishes went out. If nobody says so, they go by
themselves a quarter of an hour after the last dish was made: the food was carried long ago, or nobody is
coming for it. The dish keeps its words on the check either way.

Good to know:

- **Like every mark, it changes no dish, no price and no payment, prints nothing, and adds no step to the
  check's history.** Went out is not a sale and not a payment: the check is the same before and after.
- A drink rung in at the bar's own screen, where that screen prints no ticket at the bar (`ticket-skips.md`),
  waits for nobody's mark: the bartender who rang it makes it.
- A dish typed with no station is never "ready" (no screen lists it), and can still be sent out.
- A voided dish says it is a void and nothing else.
- The words show on a to-go order's check and a bar tab's too. Neither is on the floor map.
- It reaches an open check by itself, within a moment of the cook's tap.

## What is on a screen, and when

A screen follows the paper, rule for rule:

| | On the station's screen |
| --- | --- |
| A dish rung up and not sent | Not on it |
| A dish sent | On the screen of the station that cooks it |
| A to-go order | Its dishes at the stations that cook them, and **the whole order at the takeout station** |
| An order sent ahead for a later time | On it when its paper ticket prints, not before |
| A dish sent from a screen that prints no ticket at that station (`ticket-skips.md`) | Not on that station's screen either |
| A table's check or a bar tab that was closed | Off it: the food was eaten |
| A to-go order paid and closed | **Still on it**, until it is marked Ready |
| A to-go order marked Ready or Picked up | Off it |
| An order voided whole | Off it |
| A dish moved to another check (`moves.md`) | On the ticket of the check it is on now, made if it was made |

- More dishes sent later for the same table are **a ticket of their own**, like on paper.
- **A ticket nobody marks leaves by itself after four hours**, so a screen nobody tends does not fill up.
- Each station marks its own. The wok finishing its part of a to-go order does not take it off the takeout
  station's screen: the packer marks the bag.

## Turning it on

On the device at the station: **Settings › Kitchen screen**, tap the station it stands at, then **Save**.
Each device keeps its own.

- From then on that device shows the station's tickets whenever nobody is signed in on it, where another
  device shows the sign-in pad.
- **One screen can stand for several stations** (two woks, the fryer and the range): tap each of them. Each
  dish then says which station makes it, and a dish is made when each of its stations on that screen has
  said so; a tap says it for all of them.
- **A device is a kitchen screen or the pickup board (`pickup-board.md`), never both**: picking a station
  (or the pass) turns the board off, and turning the board on takes the stations and the pass away.
- To make the device a plain screen again: **Staff**, the gear, tap the station off, **Save**.

Set the device itself never to sleep and keep it on its charger: the app does not hold the screen awake.
(iPad: Settings › Display & Brightness › Auto-Lock › Never. Mac: System Settings › Lock Screen. Android:
Settings › Display › Screen timeout.)

## The sound

A kitchen screen chimes when a ticket comes in: two short notes, a high one and a lower one, nothing like
the phone's ring. It is what the printer's noise is to a kitchen that works off paper: the cook looks up.

- It sounds for **whatever a cook has to read anew**: a new ticket, **a dish voided or changed after it was
  sent**, and **an allergy told late**.
- It stays quiet for the kitchen's own taps (a dish made, Done, Recall) and for a ticket that only leaves.
- **Several tickets in the same moment are one chime.**
- The tickets that stand on the screen when the app starts make no sound. **What came in while somebody was
  signed in on the device chimes once when the tickets come back.**
- The pass chimes like a station's screen.
- **How loud it is, is the device's own volume**: turn it up with the device's buttons. An iPad chimes
  whatever its silent switch says; an Android tablet follows its media volume.
- **To keep one device quiet**: Settings › Kitchen screen › **Sound when a ticket comes in**, off, then
  **Save**. It is on to start on every kitchen screen. **Play it**, under the switch, plays the chime, to set
  the volume by.
- A device that is on a call does not chime: the call has the speaker.
- With the hub away no ticket arrives, so nothing chimes; when it is back, what came in meanwhile is one
  chime.

## The colours

**Location › Kitchen screens** (a manager): the minutes a ticket has been in the kitchen when it turns
amber, and when it turns red. 10 and 20 to start. 0 is never; red must come after amber. Every station's
screen follows at once.

## The staff's way in

The device is still a POS screen.

- **Staff**, at the top right, brings up the sign-in pad. A PIN signs in as on any screen, and so does a
  face where the location signs in by face: the camera looks for as long as the pad is up.
- The pad has **Kitchen screen** in its bar (an icon on a phone) to go back.
- **A pad nobody touches for a minute goes back to the tickets by itself.**
- **Signing out** puts the tickets back.
- **If the device locks itself when idle** (`idle-lock.md`), the tickets come over what was open, and a tap
  still marks a dish. Staff and a PIN (or a face, at the pad) bring back what was open.

## Good to know

- **With the hub away** the screen keeps its last picture and says "This screen may be behind: the hub is
  not answering." A dish tapped meanwhile is shown as made at once and reaches the hub when it is back;
  no tap is lost. New tickets do not arrive until then: the paper is what the kitchen cooks from.
- **The minutes** are counted on the device's own clock from the moment the dish was sent. A device whose
  clock is a minute off shows the tickets a minute older or younger.
- **A location with no printer at all** can use the screens: an order sent ahead then comes onto them by
  the clock, when its ticket would have printed.
- **The camera signs nobody in while the tickets show**, whoever stands in front of it: a cook reading the
  tickets is not signing in, and a screen that signed them in would lose its tickets to the orders. No
  picture leaves the device meanwhile. Tap **Staff** first; a sign-out, or the camera no longer seeing the
  person, puts the tickets back, and they stay. The same goes for a screen that locked itself: a face
  walking by does not unlock it.
- **A tag held to the reader** signs in straight from the tickets: that is somebody asking.
- **Leave Phone off on this device.** A call ringing shows the caller over every screen of a device that is
  a phone.
- The dishes are in the kitchen's words, as its paper has them (Chinese where the menu gives a kitchen
  name). The rest of the screen is in English.
- Not tried on the real devices from here.

## Not built

- **A screen in place of the paper**: turning a station's printer off because it has a screen.
- **A sound or a message to the server** when their food is ready: the badge and the strip are there to be
  looked at. Nor is the ready count on the list of to-go orders or of bar tabs.
- A setting for the quarter of an hour the ready mark shows.
- All-day counts by the dish with its choices, or of the whole day's sales: they count what waits now.
- **Ready by itself**: a to-go order is not marked Ready when its last dish is made; the staff tap Ready.
- A flash when a ticket comes in, a choice of chimes, a chime that repeats until the ticket is looked at, or
  a volume of its own in the app.
- A chime for a ticket that leaves because its order was voided whole.
- Text as large as a television across the room needs: the screen follows the device's own text size.
- Chinese for the words of the screen itself.

## For developers

- **The mark** is an operation on the order, `done`, sent through `POST /orders/{id}/ops` like every other
  change (`pos.sync.oplog`): `{"station": "wok", "line_ids": ["L1", "L2"]}`, with `"done": false` to take
  it back. Each line keeps, for each station, the time of its latest word (`Line.done`, in the order's
  answer as `done: {"wok": 1800000000060}`); the latest word of a station on a dish stands, whatever order
  the operations arrive in. It syncs between hubs like the rest of the log. A line that changes checks
  carries its marks with it (`pos.orders.moves.carry_ops`).
- **The pass** has a word of its own: a `done` whose `station` is `*pass` (`pos.printing.screen.PASS`; no
  station's code can be that): the dish went out. `kitchen_screen(..., passing=True)` lists the dishes of
  the stations named (of every station when none is) on one ticket, never a to-go order a second time for
  the bag; a line's `stations` is then `["*pass"]`, `done` says it went out, and `cooks` lists where it is
  cooked (`code`, `name`) and whether that station has `made` it (`TicketLine.stations` carries the
  stations out of `_ticket_lines`). On a station's screen a dish that went out counts as made
  (`pos.printing.screen._said`), unless the station's latest word is later than the pass's.
- `GET /kitchen/pass?stations=wok,fryer` (none to 32 codes; none is every station): the same answer as a
  screen's, with `"pass": true` and `cooks` on every line.
- **What the floor reads off the marks** is worked out on the hub by `pos.printing.made`: `kitchen_word`
  for a line (`cooks` with `code`, `name`, `made`; `ready_ms` once every station that cooks it has made it,
  the stations being the line's `priced.kitchen` stations less the sending screen's ticket skips; `out_ms`
  once the pass said so), `kitchen_words` for an order and `ready_of` for the floor. `GET /orders/{id}`
  puts the word on the line under `kitchen` (`with_kitchen`; `OrderState.snapshot`, which the hubs compare
  between them, does not carry it), and `GET /floor` gives each check `ready` (dishes ready and not out, by
  quantity) and `ready_ms` (when the last of them was made). A line, and a check, with nothing to say has
  no such key. Neither answer moves with the clock: the terminal counts the quarter of an hour
  (`readyShownMs`). A server's Went out is a `done` named `*pass`, like the pass's own.
- App, for the floor: `lib/kitchen_word.dart` (`KitchenWord`, `readyShownMs`, `passStation`), `Line.kitchen`,
  `withLocalOps` (a Went out still in the outbox shows at once), `TableCheck.ready`, `readyMs`, `readyAt`
  and `FloorPlan.readyAt` in `lib/floor.dart`, `_ReadyBadge` and the Mine row in
  `lib/screens/floor_view.dart`, `_ReadyStrip`, `_readyLines` and `_wentOut` in
  `lib/screens/order_screen.dart`, `lib/screens/ready_mark.dart` (the icon, the colours, the words).
- **A screen's list** is worked out on the hub by `pos.printing.screen.kitchen_screen`, with the paper's
  own routing (`Takes`, `_ticket_lines`, the ticket skips, the takeout station) and
  `PrintService.not_yet()` for the orders sent ahead whose tickets still wait.
- `GET /kitchen/screen?stations=wok,fryer` (one to eight station codes; nobody need be signed in, like the
  list of orders): `stations` (`code`, `name`), `warn` and `late` (minutes), `tickets` (the longest in the
  kitchen first) and `recent` (the last ten finished, the latest first). A ticket: `id` (the order and the
  moment its dishes reached the kitchen), `order_id`, `since_ms`, `table`, `tab`, `order_no`, `customer`,
  `to_go`, `channel`, `promised_ms`, `server`, `guests`, `course`, `allergies`, `note` (what the customer
  wrote for the whole order, on one line), `also_at`, `lines` and,
  when finished, `done_ms`. A line: `line_id`, `stations` (the ones of this screen that make it), `made`
  (the ones of them that said so), `qty`, `label`, `modifiers`, `depths`, `note`, `seat`, `header`,
  `allergy`, `void`, `changed`, `done`, `done_ms`. Nothing in the answer moves with the clock, so a screen
  that asks again is answered `304` until an order or a setting changes; the terminal counts the minutes.
- `GET /settings/kitchen_screens`, `PUT /settings/kitchen_screens` (the manager Bearer token): `{"warn":
  10, "late": 20}`, whole minutes, 0 for never, at most 1440, `late` after `warn`; one left out of a `PUT`
  keeps what it was.
- App: `lib/kitchen_screen.dart` (`KitchenBoard`, `KitchenTicket`, `KitchenLine`, `markOps`, and
  `withLocal`, which lays this device's own marks over what was read until the hub shows them;
  `KitchenBoard.counts` is All day, worked out on the device from the tickets it shows, and
  `KitchenTicket.allMade` the pass's "All made"),
  `lib/screens/kitchen_screen.dart` (`KitchenScreen`: reads on the hub's change feed and every few seconds
  without it; opens nothing over itself, since it also stands over a locked screen),
  `lib/screens/standing_gate.dart` (`StandingGate`, which stands where the sign-in pad stands and shows the
  kitchen screen, the pickup board or the pad), `Settings.kitchenStations` and `Settings.kitchenPass` (the
  preferences `kitchen_stations` and `kitchen_pass`), `lib/screens/kitchen_minutes_dialog.dart`.
- **The camera.** The face sign-in runs for the whole app (`FaceSignIn`, `face-signin.md`), not for the
  pad, so it has to be told that a standing screen is up: `FaceSignIn.standing` is true on a device that
  stands as something (`Settings.stands`) with nobody signed in and no pad on the screen, and no frame goes
  while it is. `SignInScreen` says when it is on the screen (`padUp`, `padDown`). The test is
  `apps/pos_app/test/standing_faces_test.dart`; the other tests of this screen run without a camera.
- **The sound** is the terminal's alone; the hub knows nothing of it. `lib/kitchen_bell.dart`:
  `kitchenReads` is what of a board a cook reads (each ticket's `id`, its `allergies`, and of each line its
  `qty`, `label`, `modifiers`, `note`, `allergy` and whether it is a `void`; not what is made, not the table
  or the seat), and `KitchenBell.heard` sounds once when a read brings any of it that was not on the screen
  at the look before, the finished tickets counting as seen. The bell is one for the app (`main.dart`), so
  it outlives the screen, which goes away at every sign-in; the first look at a screen is silent.
  `KitchenScreen` hands it each board the hub answers (not its own marks laid over it).
  `lib/asset_chime.dart` (`AssetChime`) plays `assets/sounds/ticket.wav`, made by `tool/make_chime.py`, with
  the system alert sound when the player refuses. `Settings.kitchenSound` is the preference `kitchen_sound`,
  kept only when it is off.
- Tests: `backend/tests/printing/test_screen.py`, `backend/tests/printing/test_made.py`,
  `apps/pos_app/test/kitchen_screen_test.dart`, `apps/pos_app/test/kitchen_pass_test.dart`,
  `apps/pos_app/test/kitchen_word_test.dart`, `apps/pos_app/test/kitchen_bell_test.dart`. The sync simulations (`pos.sync.simulate`) mark dishes made,
  and sent out, among their random operations.
