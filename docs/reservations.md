# Reservations

The reservation book: the tables booked for today and for the days ahead, in the order of their times. A
table is booked with a name, how many they are, a day and a time, and a phone number, a note and a table to
hold when there is one. When the party comes, **Seat** brings up the floor map and the next table tapped is
theirs, with the guests already filled in.

Since 18 November 2026 (the Toast comparison, row 15). Hub and app; nothing to turn on. The same day, part
two: a table booked for today says so on the floor map, and asks before somebody else is sat there
([On the floor map](#on-the-floor-map)). And part three: a table is booked from the form a call opens, and
somebody calling about a table they booked lands on it ([When somebody calls](#when-somebody-calls)). And
the last of it: the booked tables still to come are counted on the waitlist's button and said on the
waitlist ([On the waitlist's button](#on-the-waitlists-button)).

## Where it is

- **A phone**: on the orders screen, under the three dots, **Reservations**. With tables booked for today
  that are still to sit down, the row says how many: "Reservations · 4 to come".
- **Tablets and Macs**: open the waitlist (the button with the group of people in the bar at the top), then
  **Reservations** at its top right. The bar itself has no room for one more button. The book has
  **Waitlist** at its top right, so the two are one tap apart at the door. With tables booked for today,
  the waitlist has a row about them at its top, which leads to the book as well.

- **On a call**: the form that opens when a call is picked up has **Book a table** at its top right
  ([When somebody calls](#when-somebody-calls)).

Anyone signed in can open the book, book a table and seat a party. No PIN is asked: whoever answers the
phone takes the booking.

A table a guest books on the restaurant's website is written into the book by the hub itself, since 22
November 2026 ([From the website](#from-the-website)).

## Booking a table

**Book a table**, at the bottom of the book.

- **Name.** It cannot be left empty, and takes 24 letters.
- **How many guests.** A tap on 1 to 6 or 8, or minus and plus for any number up to 60. It starts at 2.
- **When.** Today, Tomorrow, or **Another day** off a calendar, up to a year ahead. Then **Time**: a grid of
  buttons, a row for each hour and a button for each quarter. It opens at five in the afternoon. Times are
  on the 24-hour clock, like the rest of the app.
- **Hold a table**, if they should have a certain one: a grid of the tables, each with its seats. A table
  that is already held close to that time says for whom ("18:30 Chen"). It can still be picked. **No
  table** takes the hold off.
- **Phone**, if they give one. Seven digits or more.
- **Note**: birthday, high chair, by the window. 80 letters.

**Book** puts it in the book of its day, and the book opens at that day. The book keeps who took the
booking and when.

A table booked for after midnight (half past twelve, one o'clock) belongs to the evening before: the book's
day runs to 4 AM, like the day's report.

## When somebody calls

Most tables are booked by phone. Picking up a call opens the to-go form with the caller's number and name
in it, as it always did (docs/customers.md). The call may be about a table and not about food:

- **Book a table**, at the top right of the form (on a phone it is the calendar with a tick). The to-go
  form closes and the reservation form opens with the **name and the number already in it**: the name from
  the customer's earlier orders when the restaurant knows the number, the phone company's name for the
  caller otherwise. Both can be typed over. Pick the guests, the day and the time, and **Book**.
- A line at the bottom says it is done ("Chen is booked for tomorrow at 18:30"), with **The book** beside
  it for a look at that day. No order is started.
- **A caller who has a table booked already** sees it on the form, under the number, as a card of two
  lines: "Tomorrow at 18:30", and under it "Table A8 booked · 4 guests". When the reservation is in
  another name than the one on the form, the card says whose it is ("Table booked for Chen · 10 guests").
  Every reservation still standing for today or a later day that was made with that number is listed, the
  soonest first. **A tap on a card** goes to the book of that day with the reservation open: Change,
  Cancel it, and for today's Seat, Here and No-show. So somebody who calls to move a table to seven or to
  say they are not coming is two taps from it.

Book a table shows while the order is **by phone**: on the form a call opens, and on a form opened with To
go once **Phone** is picked (for a call taken on a phone the app does not see). A number typed into the
form is looked up like a caller's.

While a call is on, the to-go form and the reservation form start just under the strip of the call, so the
strip (Hold, Hang up) never covers the form's title or Book a table.

Numbers are matched by their digits, so `+1 (781) 555-1234` and `781 555 1234` are the same caller. A
reservation made without a number is found by no call. A customer who ordered earlier **today** still
lands on that order when they call back, as before; the book is then reached the usual way.

## On the waitlist's button

Whoever is at the door answers two questions at once: who is waiting, and what is booked. The button of the
waitlist, in the bar at the top of the orders screen, says both.

- **The round number**, at the top right of the button, is the parties waiting, as before.
- **The square number**, at its bottom left, is the **booked tables of today that are still to come**:
  every reservation of today that still stands. One that sat down, was cancelled or was marked a no-show
  is not counted, and neither is a table booked for another day.
- The square is **plain** while every reservation is ahead, **amber** from a quarter of an hour before the
  next one's time, and **red** once one is a quarter of an hour past its time with nobody seated. Those
  are the colours the book and the floor map use. A red square says: seat them, or mark the no-show.
- With nothing booked for today there is no square, and the button is as it was.

**On the waitlist**, a row at the top says the same in words: "5 booked tables to come", and under it the
next one, its time first: "Next at 18:30: Chen, 4 guests · table A8". It reads "Here for 18:30: …" once
the party is marked Here and waits for its table, and "Due since 18:30: …" when the time is past. A tap on
the row opens the book. So a walk-in is told a wait with the book in sight.

**On a phone** the button is in the bar when it was before: while a party waits, and always for whoever
gives tables out. Booked tables alone do not put it there. The count is on the menu's row instead
("Reservations · 4 to come").

The count changes the moment a table is booked, changed, seated or called off on any screen, and starts
over when the night ends (4 AM). It is the app's part only: the hub already told every screen the book.

## Reading the book

Across the top are the days: **Today**, **Tomorrow**, then every later day that has a table booked, each
with how many ("Sat 21 Nov · 2"). **Another day** opens a calendar for any day. The line under the title
adds the day up: "Today, Mon 16 Nov · 3 reservations · 12 guests".

Each reservation is a row: how many they are in the square, the name, and under it the time, the table held
("table A7"), the guest's tags and the note.

**The tags** are the ones a manager put on that guest in the guest list ("VIP", "Catering client"), shown
as small labels (`guest-list.md`, "Where the tags show"). The book knows the guest by the **phone number**
on the reservation, so a table booked without a number shows none. They are only read here. On a narrow
phone the labels and the note go onto lines of their own under the time, so nothing is cut short.

- **The time is plain** until a quarter of an hour before it.
- **Amber** from a quarter of an hour before.
- **Red** from a quarter of an hour after it, with nobody seated: they are late, or they are not coming.
- Only today's times are coloured.

**Two reservations holding one table within an hour and a half** say so on both rows, in red: "A7 is also
held for Garcia at 19:15". The book warns and does not refuse: you may well mean it.

## A reservation's buttons

A tap on a row opens the reservation: the day and the time, the table held, who took the booking, the phone
number, the guest's tags under it and the note, over a grid of buttons.

- **Seat**: the same as the Seat button on the row (below). Today's reservations only.
- **Here**: the party has come and waits for its table. The row then says "here since 18:22", so nobody
  takes them for a no-show. The button becomes **Not here**, to take the mark off. Today only.
- **Change**: the name, the guests, the day, the time, the table, the phone, the note. Moved to another
  day, the book follows it there.
- **Cancel it**: they called to cancel.
- **No-show**: the time passed and nobody came. Today only.
- **Tags**: put a tag on this guest, or take one off ("VIP"). It asks for the PIN of somebody who may see
  the guest list, a manager unless the owner handed it out, then opens the box of tag buttons. The button
  is there when the reservation has a phone number. The guest need not have ordered
  (`guest-list.md`, "From a reservation or the waitlist"). It changes nothing about the reservation. A
  guest tagged here who has not ordered yet is in the guest list from then on, under the reservation's
  name (`guest-list.md`, "A tagged guest who has not ordered yet").

Cancel it and No-show take the reservation out of the standing ones. The bar at the bottom says so with
**Put back**, for the tap that was a slip.

## Seating a booked party

**Seat**, on the row or among the reservation's buttons. The floor map comes up with a strip across the top
("Seating Chen, party of 4. Table A7 is held for them: tap it, or another."), and the next table tapped is
theirs. **Cancel** on the strip calls it off: the reservation still stands.

It then goes as for a party off the waitlist (`waitlist.md`):

- **A free table** asks what it asks of any party, with the guests already filled in. A host or a manager
  picks the server and stays on the floor map; a server who seats the party takes the check and is taken
  into it.
- **A table that is seated** asks whether the party joins it, and adds their guests to that table's check.

The table tapped need not be the one held. The book keeps both: the table that was held and the table they
sat at, with who seated them.

**The guest's tags go to the table with them.** The check that opens for the party, or the check of the
table they join, shows the tags their number carries ("VIP") in the strip under the app bar, for whoever
serves the table. Only the tags: the name, the number and the note stay in the book, and nothing is
written onto the check (`guest-list.md`, "At the table"). **Put back** takes them off the check again.

Two screens seating the same party: the first table counts. The second screen says "Chen was already
seated at B5. The book keeps that table."

## On the floor map

**A table booked for today says so on the map**, from the morning on:

- **A free table** shows a small calendar with the time it is booked for, and under it whose it is and how
  many: "18:30", "Chen 4p". A long name is cut short.
- **A table that is seated** shows the time under its check, without the name, once the table is wanted:
  from an hour and a half before. The people sitting there are in a booked party's way. It says nothing of
  a reservation hours off: whoever sat down at three is gone before a table booked for seven.
- The time is drawn as in the book: **plain** until a quarter of an hour before it, **amber** from then,
  **red** from a quarter of an hour after it with nobody seated.
- **"here"** in place of the time: the party was marked Here and waits for this table.
- **"18:30 +1"**: the table is booked once more later today.
- Under the map, **Booked** joins the legend on a day with a table booked, on tablets and Macs. A phone's
  legend is full, and the calendar on the table says it there.
- The booking is drawn smaller sooner than it makes the table's own name smaller.

The mark goes when the party is seated (at this table or another), when the reservation is cancelled or
marked a no-show, and by itself an hour and a half after its time. The book still lists a reservation nobody
marked. A reservation with no table held is in the book and not on the map.

### Before somebody else sits there

**A free table is kept for its party from an hour and a half before its time until an hour and a half after
it.** Tapped in that time, it asks first: "A8 is booked", with the time, the party, and how far off it is
("in 25 min", "20 min ago", "here since 18:22").

- **Seat**, beside the party: these are them. The table asks the guests (filled in) and the server as for
  any party, the check opens, and the book marks them seated at that table. It is the quickest way to seat a
  booked party: tap their table.
- **Seat a walk-in**: somebody else sits there. The reservation stays in the book, and the table goes on
  saying it is booked.
- **Cancel**: the table is left alone.

A table kept for two parties (one late, one soon) names both, each with its own Seat.

The same question comes when a party off the waitlist, another booked party or a bar tab is being seated at
that table, and when a check is moved to it. It then names what is about to happen ("Seat Garcia here",
"Move A7 here") and offers no other party. A booked party seated at its own table is not asked about.

**The map warns and refuses nothing.** A table booked for eight takes a walk-in at five with no question:
they are gone by then. A table that is seated is never asked about: a tap opens its check.

## The ones that ended

Under the standing reservations is a line that counts the day's others: "2 seated · 1 cancelled ·
1 no-show". A tap opens the rows, the last to end first: "Seated at B6 · booked for 17:30 · Holly".

**Put back** on a row returns the reservation to the book as it was. For a party that was seated by
mistake, the check opened at the table stays as it is: close or void it as any check opened by mistake.

## From the website

Where online orders are on (`online-orders.md`), a table a guest books on the website is in the book by
itself once it is accepted in GloriaFood's app: the name, how many, the day and the time, the phone number,
and what the guest wrote as the note.

- It has a small **online** beside its time. A tap shows "booked on the website (GloriaFood 1234567)": the
  number to find it by in GloriaFood's app.
- From then on it is a reservation like any other: hold a table for it, change it, seat it, cancel it. No
  table is held until somebody picks one.
- A note that ends in "…" was longer than the book takes; the whole of it is in GloriaFood's app.
- A note that begins "Ordered ahead: dishes in GloriaFood." means the guest ordered their dishes with the
  table. They are **not on the POS**: ring them in from GloriaFood's app when the party sits.
- One the book could not take (it did not say for how many, or the day is full) prints a slip, NOT IN THE
  BOOK: write it in by hand.
- A guest who changes or cancels on the website afterwards does not change the book. Cancel it here.

## Good to know

- **A reservation is not a check and not a sale.** Nothing in the book is in the sales, the day's report or
  the guest list, and the book takes no money: there is no deposit. The check opened when the party sits
  down is a table's check like any other.
- **A day that is over can be read and not changed.** Pick it with Another day. A reservation nobody marked
  stays as it was.
- **Names and numbers are kept for 30 days after the day**, then taken off the hub the next time a table is
  booked. How many they were, the time and how it ended stay.
- **Nothing is sent to the guest.** The book sends no text and no e-mail, and nothing in it leaves the hub.
- **The floor map is told little.** For each table held today: the name, how many and the time. Not the
  phone number, not the note and not the guest's tags, which stay in the book. With the hub away the map shows the booked tables
  as it last read them.
- **The book is kept on the hub.** With the hub away the screen shows today's book as it was last read and
  says so; booking and changing wait for the hub. Seating still opens the check at the table, and the
  screen says the book could not be told: take the reservation out when the hub is back.
- **A change on another screen shows at once** on every screen that has the book open. So does a tag a
  manager puts on a guest, or takes off.
- **A guest's tags are not written into the book.** They are read from the guest list each time the book
  is, by the number on the reservation. A reservation that ended shows them when it is opened.
- A day takes 200 standing reservations, and a table can be booked a year ahead.
- An app that is not rebuilt has no Reservations. A hub that is not updated: the screen says the hub is
  older than reservations.

## Not built

- On the waitlist's button: how many guests the booked tables add up to, a number of its own for the late
  ones, and the button on a server's phone for booked tables alone.
- On a call: Book a table on the strip of the call itself and on an order that is already open, the
  customer's past reservations and no-shows, and a table booked by somebody whose number the phone company
  hides.
- On the map: the name on a table that is seated, the booked tables in the list beside the map while Mine
  is on, a count of late reservations in the bar over the map, and a table that says "booked" while a
  reservation is being made. How long a table is kept (an hour and a half each way) is fixed, not a setting.
- A **text or e-mail to confirm or remind**: it needs an outside service the hub does not have. (Guests
  book for themselves on the website, where online orders are on: [From the website](#from-the-website).
  A change or a cancellation they make there afterwards does not reach the book.)
- **Deposits** and card holds: payments are parked.
- A suggested table by size, and turn times that say when a table will be free.
- Standing reservations (every Friday), and a list of regulars from the book.
- A reservation going onto the guest list by itself (a guest who was tagged from one is listed; one who
  was not is not), or showing what the guest had last time. Tags from a reservation that ended, or from
  one with no phone number.
- The guest's tags on a table held on the floor map, and on the waitlist's row of booked tables. The
  reservation's name or note on the check of the table (the guest's tags are on it).
- A report of reservations, no-shows and covers over many days.

## For developers

- From the website: `Reservations.take(booking_id, name, size, at_ms, ...)` writes a table booked elsewhere
  under the id it has there (`gf-<GloriaFood's number>`), once; `Booking.online` is that number, sent as
  `online` on every booking (empty for one written on a screen). It is called by the hub alone
  (`pos.api.online`, from `pos.online.book`), never from a call on the wire. `online-orders.md` has the rest.
- Hub: `backend/src/pos/reservations.py` (`Reservations`, `Booking`, `BookStore`, `PgBookStore` with the
  table `reservation`; the rules, the limits, `day_of` and `at_of` for the day of service),
  `backend/src/pos/api/reservations.py` (`GET /reservations?day=`, `POST /reservations`,
  `PUT /reservations/{id}`, `POST /reservations/{id}/arrived`, `/seat`, `/cancel`, `/back`; each takes
  `staff_id`, answers the day's book with the reservation changed under `booking`, and tells the change
  feed `reservations`). On the wire `day` is the day of service ("2026-11-21") and `time` the restaurant's
  clock ("18:30"); a time before 04:00 is after that day's midnight. Refusals: 404 `unknown_staff`,
  `unknown_booking`, `unknown_table`; 409 `too_many`, `not_booked`, `not_today`, `day_over`; 422 `no_name`,
  `long_text`, `bad_size`, `bad_phone`, `bad_day`, `bad_time`, `past_day`, `too_far`, `bad_why`,
  `no_table`; 503 `read_only`. `soon_ms`, `late_ms` and `clash` are stamped by the hub and the app counts
  against `PosApi.nowMs()`; "today" is the hub's `today`, never the device's clock. Every reservation in
  an answer carries `tags`: the tags its number carries in the guest list, put on as the answer is made
  (`tagged`, with `pos.guest_tags.with_tags`) and stored nowhere; `guest-list.md` has the rest.
- App: `lib/reservations.dart` (`Book`, `Booking`, `BookCounts`, `bookDayWords`, `serviceMinute`,
  `serviceHours`), `lib/screens/reservations_screen.dart` (`ReservationsScreen`, pops a `SeatBooking` or an
  `OpenWaitlist`), `lib/screens/booking_dialog.dart` (`BookingDialog`, `TimeGrid`, `TablePicker`),
  `lib/screens/guests_picker.dart` (`GuestsPicker`, shared with the waitlist's form), `PosApi.reservations`,
  `addBooking`, `changeBooking`, `bookingArrived`, `seatBooking`, `cancelBooking`, `bookingBack`;
  `LiveChange.reservations`; `ArrivingParty` in `lib/waitlist.dart` is what a `WaitParty` and a `Booking`
  share, so `lib/screens/orders_screen.dart` seats either with `_seatingParty`, `_seatPartyAt` and
  `_partySeated`.
- The floor map: `Reservations.holds()` (hub) is `holds` of `GET /floor`: under each table's id, today's
  standing reservations that hold it, the earliest first, each as `Booking.as_hold()` (`id`, `name`, `size`,
  `at_ms`, `time`, `arrived_ms`, and the moments `held_ms`, `soon_ms`, `late_ms`, `until_ms`; `held_ms` and
  `until_ms` are `HOLD_MIN` before and after). The answer says nothing of the time, so it stays the same
  (304) until the book, an order or a setting changes, or a new day of service begins. App: `TableHold`,
  `FloorPlan.holds`, `holdsAt` and `keptAt` in `lib/floor.dart`; `_HoldMark` and `_holdLook` in
  `lib/screens/floor_view.dart`, which follows the clock through `_Ticking` and reads the floor again on
  `LiveChange.reservations`; `lib/screens/booked_table_dialog.dart` (`BookedTableDialog`, `holdWhenWords`,
  `holdColors`); `_bookedOk` in `lib/screens/orders_screen.dart`, asked by `_onTable`, `_seatPartyAt`,
  `_seatAt` and `_moveTo` for a free table; `Booking.ofHold` seats the party from the map.
- A call: `Reservations.of_phone()` (hub) is `GET /reservations/caller?phone=`: the standing reservations
  of today and later made with that number (compared by `phone_digits`, `MOST_OF_A_CALLER` at most), with
  `today`. App: `PosApi.callerTables` (null for a short number, no answer or an older hub), `CallerTables`
  and the words `callerTableWhen` and `callerTableWhat` in `lib/reservations.dart`; in
  `lib/screens/orders_screen.dart` the to-go form (`_TakeoutDialog`) asks beside the customer lookup,
  lists them (`_tablesCard`) and pops the `Booking` for a tapped card or a `_BookForCaller` for **Book a
  table**; `_newOrder` then calls `_reservations(day:, open:)` or `_bookForCaller`. `BookingDialog` takes
  a `name` and a `phone` to start with, and `ReservationsScreen` a `day` to open at and a reservation to
  `open`. Both forms take `underCall` and then start `callStripRoom` (`lib/screens/phone_bar.dart`) from
  the top.
- The waitlist's button: app only. `Book.toCome` (today's standing reservations; none in another day's
  book), `bookedToComeWords` and `nextBookedWords` in `lib/reservations.dart`; `BookedMark` (the square) and
  `BookedToCome` (the waitlist's row) in `lib/screens/booked_mark.dart`. `lib/screens/orders_screen.dart`
  keeps today's book (`_book`, `_readBook`: on `LiveChange.reservations`, on coming back from the book or
  the waitlist, and at most twice a minute otherwise) and rings its clock at `Book.nextChange`;
  `WaitlistScreen` takes that `book` to start with and reads it beside the list.
